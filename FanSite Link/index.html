Hannah Montana

Who is Hannah Montana?

Hannah Montana is a fictional character played by Miley Cyrus . Hannah Montana is a pop star who lives two lives. She is presented to the public eye as a blonde girl who lives a lavish popstar life. But once she's done with her popstar duties, she comes home, takes off her blonde wig, and returns to a normal teen lifestyle as Miley Ray Stewart.

What is Hannah Montana in?

  • Hannah Montana: The Movie (2009)
  • Hannah Montana (2006-2011)

More about Hannah Montana....

In the show, Hannah Montana, we get to see Miley Ray Stewart balance her popstar and teenage life style. She overcomes challenges that normal teenagers face, as well as the challenges brought up through her popstar career.
