Navient Loan Service Generates Consumer Complaints

Aidvantage, the federal loan servicing unit of Maximus Education, began handling U.S. Department of Education loans in December 2021 after Navient left the federal servicing business. Navient specifically serviced government-owned Federal Family Education Loan Program (FFELP) loans and Direct Loans.

From the Consumer Complaint Database on the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau website, the most common complaints involved poor communication with Navient and clients receiving incorrect information on loans. The states with the most frequent complaints were New York and Texas, but complaints came from across the country.

The complaints, regardless of state of origin, were tied to both federal and private student loan servicing. Navient generated a significant number of consumer complaints. The graphs and charts below illustrate Navient's complaints filed from March 16 - April 16, 2026.
